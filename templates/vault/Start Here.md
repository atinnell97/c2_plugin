Welcome — this folder is your C2 vault.

1. If Claude hasn't interviewed you yet (`99 System/Context.md` is
   blank), ask Claude to **onboard you** — it personalizes the vault:
   your role, duties, projects, and meeting series.
2. Open this folder as a vault in Obsidian (File → Open folder as
   vault). Obsidian is the human window; Claude is the librarian.
   One-time Obsidian setting: enable the core **Templates** plugin
   (Settings → Core plugins) and set its folder to `Templates` — that
   makes "new note from template" work when you file things by hand.
   (Claude fills templates itself either way.)
3. From then on, just work: hand Claude meeting transcripts, ask it to
   triage your email, capture loose thoughts into `00 Inbox`.

The About note in each folder explains what lives there.
`99 System/Conventions.md` is the human rulebook;
`99 System/Contract.md` is the machine one. You never need to memorize
either — Claude reads them so you don't have to.

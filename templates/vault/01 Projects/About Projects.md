Work with an end state lives here — a work order, a build, a
migration. The test: does it finish? Each project is a folder with
a Home note (use the template) carrying its status and cadence.
Everything scoped to a project — meetings, decisions, refresh logs —
lives inside it and archives with it in one move when it's done.
Related projects may share one grouping folder (Client Engineering/),
one level deep, two-project minimum. A slow monthly heartbeat still
counts as moving; status lives in the Home note, not the location.
